- **Scenario**: both creatures start 30 feet apart. The name is the furthest apart they may get: `max30` 30 ft, `max120` 120 ft, `max600` 600 ft.
- **Rank**: position by Elo within the scenario, 1 is strongest.
- **Elo**: strength fitted to all pairings at once, scaled so the median CR 1 creature is 1000. A 400-point gap is 10 to 1 odds.
- **Win**: the creature's average score over its 329 opponents, each opponent counting equally.
- Rows are ordered by the mean of the 3 ranks. `README.md` in this folder defines every column exactly.
