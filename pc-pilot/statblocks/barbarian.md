# Barbarian (Berserker)

Benchmark stat blocks at levels 1, 5, 11, 17, as the duel simulator fights them. Generated by `node pc-pilot/markdown.mjs` from `classes.mjs`; do not edit.

How to read them: limited uses are for the one fight, with every slot and use available. A spell printed under Actions is the same spell as in the list, with a class feature's bonus in its dice: it is marked with the slot it spends, and that slot is shared with the other spells of its level. "Simulator notes" list what the text alone does not show. "Duels like CR" is the monster CR at which the block's mean score in the last `max120` ladder first drops below one half and stays below at the next CR; one CR either way is noise, and more than that at level 17. Conventions and what is left out are in `../README.md`.

### Traits

***Relentless Rage.*** If the character drops to 0 Hit Points while raging and doesn't die outright, it can make a DC 10 Constitution saving throw (DC 5 higher each later use). On a success, its Hit Points instead change to twice its Barbarian level. Modelled as one reset to 81 Hit Points.

### Actions

***Multiattack.*** The character makes two attacks, using Maul or Javelin in any combination.

***Maul.*** *Melee Attack Roll:* +11, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 12 (2d6 + 5) Bludgeoning damage plus 4 Bludgeoning damage while raging, and the target must succeed on a DC 19 Constitution saving throw or have the Prone condition.

***Javelin.*** *Melee or Ranged Attack Roll:* +11, reach 5 ft. or range 30/120 ft. *Hit:* 8 (1d6 + 5) Piercing damage plus 4 Piercing damage while raging.

***Rage (6/Day).*** The character enters a rage that lasts for 10 minutes. While raging, it has resistance to Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing damage.

***Intimidating Presence (6/Day).*** *Wisdom Saving Throw:* DC 19, one creature within 30 feet that the character can see. *Failure:* The target has the Frightened condition for 1 minute. At the end of each of its turns, the target repeats the save, ending the effect on itself on a success.

### Reactions

***Retaliation.*** *Trigger:* The character takes damage from a creature within 5 feet of it. *Response:* The character makes one Maul attack against that creature.

### Simulator notes

- Reckless Attack is always on: Advantage on every attack roll, and the AC shown is half plate's 16 less 4 for the Advantage it gives the foe.
- Frenzy: the first Maul hit of each turn while raging deals 4d6 + 5 more Bludgeoning damage (the +5 is Improved Brutal Strike's net worth over keeping Advantage).
- Danger Sense is +4 on Dexterity saves.
- Feral Instinct is +3 Initiative.
- Relentless Rage is one reset to 81 Hit Points, the expected total of its repeated saves; it is not tied to raging.
- Intimidating Presence: one free use and five more from spare Rage uses. The planner rarely or never chooses it.
